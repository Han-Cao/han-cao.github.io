I am a computational geneticist working on genomics and population genetics, with a focus on the genetics of Alzheimer's disease.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow in Prof. [Nancy Ip](https://iplab.hkust.edu.hk/)'s lab at [the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/), where I also received my Ph.D. in Life Science under her supervision. Before that, I obtained my B.S. in Biological Science from [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/).
